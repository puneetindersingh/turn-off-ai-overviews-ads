# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name
Turn Off AI Overviews & Ads on Google

## Short description (≤132 chars)
Turn off Google AI Overviews and ads instantly. Hide, disable or remove AI results and sponsored ads. Fast, private, no tracking.

## Detailed description
Tired of scrolling past ads and AI Overviews to reach real search results? Clean SERP removes them instantly.

WHAT IT DOES
• Hides AI Overviews — the AI-generated answer block at the top of results
• Hides ads — top & bottom text ads, shopping/product ads, sidebar ads, and inline "Sponsored" results
• Two simple toggles in the toolbar popup — turn either off anytime

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
• Flash-free: ads are hidden before the page paints, so you never see them flicker
• Blazing fast: built almost entirely on CSS — no heavy scripts slowing your searches
• Private by design: zero tracking, zero analytics, zero network requests. Nothing ever leaves your browser
• Minimal permissions: runs only on Google Search — no access to your other tabs or browsing

Works across 45+ Google country domains. Dark-mode aware popup.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Open source. No account required.

• host permissions (Google Search domains) — required to inject the CSS/JS that hides ads and AI Overviews on the search results page. No other sites are accessed.
